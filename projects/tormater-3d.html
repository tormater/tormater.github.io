NOTICE: Tormater 3D has been replaced by Tormater 2: Electric Boogaloo, so go check that out instead!
Tormater 3D is a snazzy first-person shooter with the added bonus of Tormater. It's lightweight, fun, and runs on any computer from the last decade or so.
But most importantly, Tormater 3D is filled to the brim with over 50 times as much Tormater as your average videogame product.
That's right, over 50 times. We're talking Tormater here.

Tormater 3D is a standalone game and an addon for Doom which replaces most of the content in the game with Tormater related objects. This means you can use Tormater 3D alongside the level pack of your choice.

Tormater 3D brings hours of interesting and exciting gameplay for years to come. From firing a stream of ampersands into a crowd of enemies, to dodging the Amazing Flying Tormen, in this action-packed, fun-filled adventure.
