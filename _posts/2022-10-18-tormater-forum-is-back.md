After its continued absence due to issues regarding spam management and board administration, Tormater Forum is back online. Tormater Forum is now running the in-house developed Tormater Forum software, so the board has been wiped to a fresh new clean slate. Due to personal and ethical issues regarding the previous administration of the forum, Tormater Forum's revival has taken us longer than we had expected.

We know this has been long awaited, and we are also excited to announce that Tormater Forum is now under new administration, and will be managed and lead by John Shepard, or as he is known in the Tormater community, tormater-yak.

Thank you for your patience and we hope you enjoy the bright future of Tormater Forum.
