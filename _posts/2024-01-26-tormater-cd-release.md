Have you been playing Doom and have been disappointed by the serenity of the levels? Do you need something that will scratch that Tormater itch? Try Tormater CD, a dynamic Tormaterizer for Doom that will run over any mod/game in the Doom engine. You'll be swimming in heaps and heaps of Tormater. 

Recommended: For the maximum TORMATER experience, run the pk3 alongside Tormater 2: Electric Boogaloo.

Features:

-    Dynamically Tormatered enemies with custom behavior
-    Monsters and objects are now free to speak their mind in the Doom chatbox. (PS: All they talk about is Tormater.)
-    Levels are now --> hooked --> into the Tormater and will undergo amazing changes.
-    And much, much more. tor_GET today!

Controls:

-    Move camera: Mouse
-    Fire weapon: LMB
-    Secondary fire weapon: RMB
-    Change weapon: Mouse Scroll
-    Move character: W, A, S, and D
-    Use switches/open doors: Space
-    Pause game: ESC 

System Requirements:

-    Dual-core 2.4GHz 64-bit processor
-    A dedicated GPU from 2012 or later, or integrated from 2016 or later
-    4 GBs of RAM
-    50 MBs of storage space (for game and save data) 

Operating System Requirements (for pre-compiled binaries):

-    Windows Vista or newer
