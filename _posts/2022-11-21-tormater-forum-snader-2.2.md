Release snader-2.2

-    Full lingualization of the forum software (this means that Languages will be able to translate any text on the site)
-    URLs are now generated by the site using a baseURL (this means that you can install the forum onto any page of your site, not just the root)
-    Deleting the last post in a thread will now delete the entire thread
