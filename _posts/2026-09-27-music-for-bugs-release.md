We here at Tormater.com have released a new EP for the masses, *music for bugs*, after many many dozens hours of back-breaking tiring work. After all, to truly make music for bugs, you must think like a bug, which is harder than you may think--or rather assume.

 To properly immerse yourself in the soundscape of *music for bugs*, you must put yourself into the many shoes that a bug wears. **Fact:** Centipedes have to wear over 99 pairs of shoes. Try walking a mile in that!
