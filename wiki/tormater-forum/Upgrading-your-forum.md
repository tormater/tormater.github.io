---
title: Upgrading your forum
---
Upgrading your forum is easy and simple on Tormater Forum.

First, download the [latest release](https://github.com/tormater/tormater-forum/releases/latest) or the [latest dev build](https://github.com/tormater/tormater-forum/archive/refs/heads/main.zip) of the software, and upload it to your webserver. Be sure to override old files, otherwise the upgrade will not work.<br/>
Next, go to the upgrade page (`yourforumurl.here/upgrade/`) on your forum, to complete the update, and your forum will be up-to-date.
