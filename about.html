About us

Tormater Yak
Tormater Yak is a developer and creator of Tormater Forum, and a widely known member of the Tormater Community, and administrator on our community forum.
Alfred Don
Alfred Don is a highly influential member of the Tormater Community, and is a creator of numerous Tormater video games.
Alfred is also an administrator and current owner of the Tormater discord guild.
GigaHacer
GigaHacer is a freelance software engineer, and was an influential developer on the Tormater Forum project,
and former administrator of the community forum.
vegbao
Vegbao is a Taiwanese developer of Tormater Forum.
Vegbao hosts a personal website, which can be found at vegbao.com
cosmopolityan
cosmopolityan is a frontend web developer.
They are a contributor to the Tormater Forum project, having created the Russian language pack.
Abdulhalim
Abdulhalim is a Iranian developer of Tormater Forum, who is responsible for creating the Persian language pack, as well as RTL variants of Tormater Forum's themes.
Abdulhalim hosts a personal website at pourdaryaei.ir.
Gordon Beanman/FloatingBeans
Gordon Beanman (or FloatingBeans, Beans6650, AZURED) is a member of the Tormater community who has created the Hindi language pack for Tormater Forum, as well as games, and music for the Tormater project.
GordonBeanman hosts a personal website at floatingbeans.github.io.
