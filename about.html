About us

Tormater Yak
Tormater Yak is a developer and creator of Tormater Forum, and a widely known member of the Tormater Community, and administrator on our community forum.
Alfred Don
Alfred Don is a highly influential member of the Tormater Community, and is the sole creator of all of the Tormater games to-date.
Alfred is also an administrator and current owner of the Tormater discord guild.
GigaHacer
GigaHacer is a freelance software engineer, and an influential developer on the Tormater Forum project,
and administrator on our community forum.
vegbao
Vegbao is a Taiwanese developer of Tormater Forum. Vegbao hosts a personal website at vegbao.com
cosmopolityan
cosmopolityan is a 27 year old frontend web developer.
He is a contributor to the Tormater Forum project, and a student at Yandex.Praktikum - web development department.
Abdulhalim
Abdulhalim is a Iranian developer of Tormater Forum.
He has created the Persian language pack and the two RTL variants of Tormater Forum's themes.
Abdulhalim hosts a personal website at pourdaryaei.ir.
FloatingBeans
FloatingBeans is a member of the Tormater community who is currently translating Tormater Forum into Hindi.
FloatingBeans is also a new developer of the Tormater video game series.
